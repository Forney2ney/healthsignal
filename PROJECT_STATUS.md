## Known limitations (current)

- No GPU (CPU-only) — all training is small-tabular or pretrained-image inference.
- No external validation for any model; all metrics are cohort-specific.
- Scores are uncalibrated research indicators, not validated individual probabilities.
- The imaging model was not re-validated locally; it accepts chest X-rays only.
- scikit-learn 1.9.1 compiled extensions are blocked by a Windows Application Control policy in the
  user-wide site-packages; everything runs from the project `.venv` (D-002).
- CKD zip wraps a `.rar` requiring WinRAR/7-Zip (D-008); 1 malformed source row excluded (399 of 400).
- Official hackathon rules/brief not present in repo; project designed to the criteria in
  `HEALTHSIGNAL_PROJECT.md`.
- Frontend E2E is exercised via the documented demo script rather than automated browser tests.
