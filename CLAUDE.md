# HealthSignal project guidance

Read `HEALTHSIGNAL_PROJECT.md` before making significant changes. Follow
it as the persistent project specification, including its scope,
engineering workflow, safety and privacy boundaries, 24-hour
prioritisation, testing standards and status reporting.

Inspect existing work before editing. Keep `PROJECT_STATUS.md` and
`DECISIONS.md` current. Never present simulated or untested
functionality as real, and never fabricate medical or model-evaluation
claims.
